
# My Favorite Movies

Here are my five favorite movies:

1. **Avengers: Endgame**
   - A superhero movie about the Avengers fighting to save the universe.

2. **Spider-Man: No Way Home**
   - A movie about Spider-Man dealing with the consequences of revealing his identity.

3. **The Lion King**
   - An animated movie about a young lion who learns to become king.

4. **Inside Out**
   - An animated movie about emotions and growing up.

5. **Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone**
   - A fantasy movie about a young wizard discovering his magical abilities.
